# Results

Everything in this directory is evidence. Two kinds of file live here, and they are treated
differently.

## Ours — kept in English

Reports we wrote about our own measurements: `bench-qa.md` (generated by
`bench-qa.mjs`), `e1-agent-browser.md`, `e2-contracts.md`, `e4-where-they-win.md`,
`e5-coexistence.md`, `abis-verbs.md`, `c-false-green.md`, `claude-vs-chrome.md`, and the
files under `../formal/results/`.

## Somebody else's — kept in the original language, unedited

`codex-self.md`, `codex-self-v2.md` … `codex-self-v5.md`, `codex-mcp.md`,
`codex-assert.md`, `codex-f3-privacy.md`.

These are the reports an outside reviewer wrote in the first person about our tool,
including the parts where it came off badly. **They are not translated and not edited.**
Rewriting somebody else's assessment — even only into another language — turns primary
evidence into our paraphrase of it, and the whole value of those rounds is that we did not
write them. Some are in Spanish and some in English, depending on the round; that is how
they arrived.

If a claim from one of those reports matters, quote it and say it is a quotation, rather
than folding it into our own prose.

## The JSON files

Raw per-case data next to each report, gitignored except where a report cites it. Regenerate
with the command listed at the bottom of the matching report.
